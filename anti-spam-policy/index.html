LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Anti-Spam Compliance

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Permission-Based Communication

Frugal With Seth aims to send relevant communications only to recipients who have requested them or where another lawful basis for contact applies. We do not use purchased or scraped marketing contact lists or intentionally deceptive sender names or subject lines.

Email Newsletters

Subscribers may receive travel tips, current flight deal information, and new video notifications. Marketing emails should identify the sender and include an effective unsubscribe method, as well as required sender contact details. You can also email frugalwithseth@gmail.com to request removal.

Text Messages

Promotional SMS messages require appropriate opt-in consent. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We honor valid opt-out requests and do not intentionally resume promotional messages without renewed permission. See our SMS & Opt-In Policy.

Handling Preferences and Complaints

We manage subscription and opt-out preferences through our communications systems, including GoHighLevel / LeadConnector where applicable. Questions, concerns, or complaints about messages can be sent to frugalwithseth@gmail.com.

Service Versus Marketing Messages

Messages answering a direct inquiry or providing a requested operational update are different from promotional campaigns. We do not use an operational message as blanket permission for unrelated marketing.

Related Information

For details about data use, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
