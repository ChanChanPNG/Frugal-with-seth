SMS & Opt-In Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Sender and Program

Frugal With Seth may send SMS messages to people who have chosen to receive them. Depending on what an individual signs up for, messages may include travel or flight deal alerts, new video announcements, replies to inquiries, and other expressly requested communications. Promotional and informational messages should be separately authorized where applicable.

How You Opt In

Providing a phone number does not automatically subscribe you to promotional texts. SMS consent must be collected through a clear opt-in mechanism that describes the sending brand and message purpose, such as an optional unchecked checkbox on a form. Consent is not a condition of purchase. The exact opt-in options presented to you at sign-up govern your subscription.

Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile plan.

Stop or Get Help

Reply STOP to a text message to request cancellation. Reply HELP for support, or email frugalwithseth@gmail.com. You may also request withdrawal of consent by contacting us. Carrier delivery is not guaranteed and may depend on network or carrier conditions.

Consent and Privacy

We keep records of communication preferences and opt-in status where collected. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in information and consent are not shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing. Service providers may process data solely to deliver and administer our messages. Review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Important Program Clarification

The existence of this policy does not enroll visitors in SMS messages. Text messaging may only be initiated through a configured consent and messaging workflow appropriate to the message type.
