Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who We Are

Frugal With Seth operates this website and publishes travel-related videos, tips, and newsletter content. For privacy questions, contact frugalwithseth@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us, we may receive details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, message, and communication preferences. The website may also collect technical information such as device type, browser details, pages visited, referral sources, and cookie or analytics identifiers when tracking tools are active.

How We Use Information

We use information to deliver requested flight-deal and video-release newsletters; respond to inquiries; maintain records of messaging preferences and consent; operate, secure and improve our website; measure website usage; and send text messages when we have the appropriate permission.

Services and Disclosures

Our newsletter form uses GoHighLevel / LeadConnector, which processes submitted information to support our communications. Website hosting and other technical providers may handle data as needed to operate the site. Embedded content, including YouTube videos, may collect data under its provider’s own privacy terms. We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Information may be made available to service providers solely to support our communications and operations, or disclosed if required by law.

Analytics and Cookies

The website may use analytics and similar technologies to understand visits and improve content. Such technologies may process device or usage information. Embedded third-party content may independently set cookies or track interactions. Your browser or device settings may allow you to limit certain cookies. The specific analytics services and cookie controls should be checked against the deployed website.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described, including managing subscriptions, responding to requests, documenting consent, and meeting applicable obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices and Requests

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe option in the message, and withdraw SMS consent by replying STOP. To ask about accessing, correcting, or deleting personal information, email frugalwithseth@gmail.com. We will evaluate requests in accordance with applicable law.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The current version will be published here with its last-updated date.
